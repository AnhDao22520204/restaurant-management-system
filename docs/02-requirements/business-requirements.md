# Business Requirements

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| BR-001 | The restaurant shall use a centralized system to support core operational management rather than relying only on manual handling. |
| BR-002 | The restaurant shall manage authenticated user access and role-dependent functions. |
| BR-003 | The restaurant shall manage tables and their operating status for customer service/order handling. |
| BR-004 | The restaurant shall manage employee information and employee-related administration. |
| BR-005 | The restaurant shall manage menu/food information used in restaurant operations. |
| BR-006 | The restaurant shall manage ingredient inventory and inbound/outbound stock records. |
| BR-007 | The restaurant shall support customer food ordering linked to table and invoice/order details. |
| BR-008 | The restaurant shall support invoice creation, payment handling, invoice status, and invoice history. |
| BR-009 | The restaurant shall support financial/revenue statistics for management review. |
| BR-010 | The system shall support management decision-making through recorded operational and financial information. |
