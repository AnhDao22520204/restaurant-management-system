# Business Problem

The report identifies several operational problems that motivate a restaurant management system:

1. Large volumes of reservations/orders can be difficult to manage accurately and may create conflicts.
2. Order and payment processing requires speed and accuracy, including correct totals and invoice creation.
3. Employee information, schedules, attendance, salary, and performance-related administration require structured management.
4. Management needs business reports on revenue, profit, and other operating information to support decisions.

The broader problem is that manual handling increases effort, error risk, and coordination difficulty.
