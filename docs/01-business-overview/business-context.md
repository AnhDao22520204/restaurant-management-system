# Business Context

The source report describes a restaurant environment where manual handling of ordering, payment, employee operations, revenue, and related activities can cause errors and operational inefficiency. The proposed system is intended to centralize these activities digitally.

The report describes intended use on laptops, tablets, and phones, with authenticated accounts and role-dependent access. Staff can place customer orders, order information is intended to reach the kitchen, invoices are retained after payment, and revenue is summarized periodically.


## Main operational areas described

- Food ordering
- Table management
- Payment and invoice handling
- Employee administration
- Inventory / ingredients
- Revenue and financial reporting
