# Project Objectives

The report defines the overall objective as building a restaurant management system to optimize management processes, improve business effectiveness, and improve service quality.

## Documented objectives

- Reduce management time and effort.
- Automate selected management activities such as scheduling and invoice printing.
- Provide detailed operating/business statistics.
- Improve business efficiency and profitability.
- Improve service speed and customer satisfaction.
- Complete the project within three months.
- Make the system easy to use and suitable for restaurant needs.
- Ensure data safety and security.
